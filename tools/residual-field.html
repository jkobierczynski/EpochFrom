Residual Field

Per-star tangent-plane residuals (ξ = RA-like, η = Dec-like, milliarcsec) from a calibrate --residuals-csv export — laid out across the sensor to tell a radially-symmetric cause (field curvature) apart from one tied to a mount axis or a single sub (tracking, guiding).

Example dataset

u/v are pixel offsets from the polynomial's reference pixel (crpix). ξ/η are the RA-/Dec-direction sky residuals at that star, in milliarcseconds.

Spatial vector field

Each arrow starts at a star's sensor position and points toward its residual, scaled for visibility. A pattern that grows outward from the centre (longer arrows near the edge, roughly radial direction) is the signature of field curvature; a pattern that doesn't care about position on the sensor at all is not optical.

Residual by axis

Distribution of ξ and η separately. A wide gap between the two RMS values points at a cause tied to one mount axis (periodic error in RA, polar-alignment drift in Dec) rather than the optics.

ξ · RA-like η · Dec-like

Residual vs. radius

Magnitude of each residual against distance from crpix, with the binned mean overlaid. A rising trend supports field curvature; a flat one argues against it.

Per-sub agreement

Mean ξ/η offset and RMS for each sub. A single sub standing out (not the whole session) usually means that exposure specifically — a guiding hiccup, a gust, a passing cloud — not the rig.

Reads the CSV EpochFrom calibrate … --residuals-csv <path> writes: sub_index, pixel_u, pixel_v, radius_px, dxi_before_mas, deta_before_mas, dxi_after_mas, deta_after_mas, kept. Points parsed entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. This export doesn't carry a per-star identity across subs, so it can't reconstruct the internal (sub-to-sub) repeatability-by-axis number the CLI report prints separately; the per-sub panel here is the closest available view from this file. Large sessions: the vector field and radius scatter show a random sample for legibility, everything else (stats, histograms, per-sub bars) uses every row.
